How we work

The Ascent

Every engagement follows the same three moves.

01 · CHART

Find the steepest path

We scope the highest-leverage problem, define what “better” means, and build the evals before we build the system. No slideware: week one produces measurements, not documents.

02 · CLIMB

Ship early, then harden

A working system reaches production in weeks, wrapped in the engineering it needs to stay there: observability, CI/CD, durable execution, cost and latency control.

03 · COMPOUND

Make it self-improving

Feedback loops, eval-gated agents, and automated retraining keep the system climbing after we hand it over. The goal is a system that gets better without us, and a team that knows exactly how it works.

One accountable senior lead per engagement, drawing on a network of specialist engineers when the problem calls for it. Everyone who touches your system has shipped AI to production before.
